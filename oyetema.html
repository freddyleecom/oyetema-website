About the House

Craft Born
from Continent

OYETEMA is a luxury fashion house rooted in the textures, colors, and stories of the African continent. Founded by designer Temi Oyedeji, the house blends ancestral craft traditions with the rigour of contemporary couture.

Each collection is shaped by months of research, travel, and conversation with artisan communities — from Kente weavers in Bonwire to Kanga pattern-makers along the East African coast. The result is fashion that remembers where it came from.

Our Atelier

Where Thread
Meets Truth

"A garment that does not carry a story is simply cloth. We dress you in memory."

— Temi Oyedeji, Creative Director

I

Heritage Fabric

We source directly from artisan weavers across West, East, and Southern Africa, ensuring the traditions that shaped each textile are respected, compensated, and celebrated.

II

Slow Fashion

Every collection is limited. We release two seasons per year and never manufacture excess. Scarcity is not a sales strategy — it is a commitment to intention.

III

Exacting Craft

Our Accra atelier employs 30 master tailors who apprentice for a minimum of three years before touching a single OYETEMA garment. Perfection is our baseline.

IV

Personal Fit

Ready-to-wear pieces are made in 12 precise size variations. Every purchase includes one complimentary alteration session at any of our three atelier locations.
